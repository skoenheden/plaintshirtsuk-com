What is a plain V-neck T-shirt good for?

A V-neck is a T-shirt with a V-shaped opening at the front instead of a round collar. Everything else, sleeves, body and fabric, is the same as a classic tee. The neckline is what changes the look.

  • Under an open shirt: the V stays hidden behind the collar, so no undershirt shows.
  • Under a blazer or cardigan: a cleaner, slightly dressier line than a crew neck.
  • Warm days: the open neckline feels a little cooler.
  • Body shape: the V lengthens the neck and balances broad shoulders.

How deep should the V be?

For a plain everyday tee, a shallow to medium V of approx. 7 to 10 cm from the collarbone works for most people. It shows a little more than a crew neck without looking like a fashion statement.

A deep V of approx. 12 cm or more is a style choice and harder to wear under a shirt, because it shows at the collar. If the tee is mainly an undershirt, go for a shallow V.

Which fabric should a plain V-neck have?

Cotton is the safe choice for a tee worn close to the skin. It is soft, breathable and keeps the neckband stable when it has a ribbed edge. Frequently asked questions

Are V-neck T-shirts still in style?
Yes, as long as the V is moderate. Very deep V-necks have gone out of fashion, but a shallow to medium V is a classic basic that works under a blazer, an open shirt or a cardigan. A plain V-neck in white, grey or navy is one of the easiest tees to dress up.
Who should wear a V-neck instead of a crew neck?
A V-neck suits people with a rounder face, a shorter neck or broad shoulders, because the V draws the eye downwards and makes the neck look longer. A crew neck suits slim builds and long necks better. If you wear the tee under an open-collar shirt, a V-neck keeps the neckline hidden.
How do I stop a V-neck from stretching?
Wash it inside out at 30 to 40 degrees and skip the hot tumble dryer. Do not pull the tee over your head by the neckband, and hang it to dry rather than on a hook by the collar. A ribbed neckband in cotton holds its shape much better than a simple folded edge.
What size V-neck should I choose?
Choose the same size as your usual crew neck tee. Measure your chest under your arms and compare it with the size chart. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one, as cotton can shrink a few percent on a hot first wash. The best plain T-shirts guide covers fit in more detail.
