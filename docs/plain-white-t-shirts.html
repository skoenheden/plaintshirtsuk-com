Quick answer

What makes a good plain white T-shirt?

A white tee is the one basic that shows every weakness. Thin fabric looks see-through, a weak collar goes wavy and grey patches appear under the arms after a few months. A good one has three things going for it:

  • Enough fabric weight so it is not see-through.
  • A ribbed collar that lies flat and holds its shape.
  • Cotton that can take regular washing without losing its colour.

How heavy should a white T-shirt be so it is not see-through?

Opacity depends mainly on fabric weight, measured in gsm (grams per square metre). A dense knit lets less light through. As a rough guide:

What should you wear under a white T-shirt?

Surprisingly, a white vest under a white tee often shows through more than nothing at all, because it creates a visible outline. A layer close to your skin tone is the least visible. For a plain white tee on its own, heavier fabric is the simplest fix.

How do you keep white T-shirts white?

Most white tees do not go grey or yellow from age, but from washing habits. These five habits make the biggest difference:

  1. Wash whites separately. Even one dark or coloured item can give white a grey tint over time.
  2. Use a whites detergent and the right dose. Too much detergent leaves residue that attracts dirt.
  3. Wash at 30 to 40 degrees for everyday loads. Hotter can remove more grime, but cotton may shrink a few percent.
  4. Treat sweat marks early. Deodorant and sweat build up under the arms and at the collar and turn yellow if left.
  5. Dry in fresh air where you can, and avoid a hot tumble dryer, which can set stains and shrink cotton.

Crew neck, V-neck or long sleeve: which white basic do you need?

A white tee is not one garment but several, depending on how you wear it. Most wardrobes benefit from a mix:

  • White crew neck: the classic. It works on its own with jeans, under a jumper or with a blazer for a smart-casual look.
  • White V-neck: useful under an open shirt, as the collar stays out of sight. Many people also find it a little cooler in summer.
  • White long sleeve: a base layer for autumn and winter, or on its own on cooler days.

For the crew neck you wear on its own, a heavier fabric pays off most, because nothing is covering it. For an undershirt or V-neck worn under something else, a lighter tee is often more comfortable.

White is also the most common colour for printing, from club logos to hen do tees. If you plan to print, cotton of approx. 150 to 190 gsm gives a smooth, stable surface. Classic Fruit of the Loom tees are tubular, with no side seams, so the fabric is even all the way round.

Do white cotton T-shirts shrink?

Cotton can shrink a few percent, mostly on the first hot wash. Choose your normal size and, if you are between two sizes, go for the larger one. Approx. chest measurements for Fruit of the Loom tees: S 89 to 94 cm, M 95 to 100 cm, L 101 to 106 cm, XL 107 to 112 cm.

How many white T-shirts should you buy at once?

White tees wear out faster than dark ones, simply because they show marks sooner. Many people buy three to five at a time so they can rotate them. That also fits well with quantity discounts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I stop a white T-shirt being see-through?
Choose a heavier fabric. Tees of approx. 140 to 150 gsm can be slightly see-through in white, while approx. 165 gsm and up is noticeably more opaque and heavy cotton of approx. 190 gsm is the safest choice. A skin-toned layer underneath also helps more than a white vest, which tends to show through.
Why do white T-shirts go yellow?
Yellowing usually comes from sweat and deodorant building up in the fabric, especially under the arms and at the collar. Too much detergent, hot drying and storing tees slightly damp also play a part. Wash whites promptly after wearing, use a whites detergent and avoid overdosing.
Can I wash white T-shirts at 60 degrees?
Cotton can usually handle it, but always check the care label first. A hotter wash removes more grime, yet it also increases the risk of shrinking a few percent, especially on the first wash. For everyday washing 30 to 40 degrees with a good whites detergent is usually enough.
