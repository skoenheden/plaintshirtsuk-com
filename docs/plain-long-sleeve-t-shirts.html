What is a plain long sleeve T-shirt good for?

A long sleeve tee is simply a T-shirt with full-length sleeves. It is made from the same soft jersey as a short sleeve tee, so it feels familiar, but it covers more when the weather turns.

  • Layering: under a jumper, a flannel shirt or an open hoodie.
  • Base layer: under a jacket on cold mornings, for walking, cycling or work outdoors.
  • On its own: on mild spring and autumn days, when short sleeves are too little.
  • Printing: a sleeve print or a small chest logo for staff and events.

Which fabric should a plain long sleeve tee have?

Cotton is the safest choice for a long sleeve worn close to the skin. It is soft, breathable and does not trap sweat the way polyester can. The Fruit of the Loom Valueweight Long Sleeve T-shirt is 100 % cotton and certified to OEKO-TEX Standard 100, which means the fabric has been tested for harmful substances.

Valueweight is Fruit of the Loom's mid-weight range. The short sleeve Valueweight tee weighs approx. 165 gsm, a good all-round weight: substantial enough to hold its shape, light enough to wear under a jumper. The T-shirt gsm guide explains how light, mid and heavy tees differ.

How should a long sleeve T-shirt fit?

  1. Sleeves: the cuff should reach your wrist bone when your arms hang down.
  2. Cuffs: ribbed cuffs keep the sleeves in place when you push them up.
  3. Shoulders: the shoulder seam should sit at the edge of your shoulder, not halfway down the arm.
  4. Length: the hem should cover your belt, so it stays tucked under a jumper.

Compare your chest measurement with the size guide rather than relying on the letter on the label. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one, as cotton can shrink a few percent on a hot first wash. The best plain T-shirts guide has the chest measurements for Fruit of the Loom tees.

How do you keep a long sleeve tee in shape?

  • Wash at 30 to 40 degrees, inside out, with similar colours.
  • Skip the hot tumble dryer, which can shrink cotton and make the sleeves shorter.
  • Dry it flat or on a hanger so the cuffs and collar keep their shape.
  • Wash white and light tees separately to keep them bright, as explained in plain white T-shirts.

Frequently asked questions

What is a plain long sleeve T-shirt made of?
Most plain long sleeve tees are made of cotton jersey, the same knit as a short sleeve tee. The Fruit of the Loom Valueweight Long Sleeve T-shirt sold by Marmalade Co. is 100 % cotton and certified to OEKO-TEX Standard 100. Heather colours from other brands often contain some polyester, which is normal.
Should a long sleeve T-shirt fit tighter than a short sleeve?
Not necessarily. Choose the same size you wear in a short sleeve tee if you want it on its own or over a thin base layer. If it will mainly be worn under a shirt or jumper, a closer fit avoids bunching. Check the sleeve length too: the cuff should reach your wrist bone.
Which colours are available?
At Marmalade Co. the Fruit of the Loom long sleeve tee comes in 6 colours: white, black, navy, royal blue, grey and dark grey. That covers the classic basics for layering. Short sleeve tees come in 15 colours if you need a wider choice, for example for a team or event.
Is a long sleeve T-shirt warm enough on its own in autumn?
On mild days, yes. A cotton long sleeve tee covers the arms and keeps the chill off, but it is still a single layer of jersey. When it gets colder, wear it under a sweatshirt, hoodie or jacket. That way you can take a layer off indoors and still be covered.
