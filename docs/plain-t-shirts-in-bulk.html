How do you split sizes for a group order?

Which colours work best for group T-shirts?

Colour is often decided quickly, but it affects how the tees look after a day of wear and a few washes. A few points to think about for groups:

  • White: clean and easy to print on, but shows marks quickly and should be heavy enough not to be see-through. See plain white T-shirts.
  • Black and navy: smart, forgiving and good for staff and events. Dark tees last longest when washed inside out at 30 degrees.
  • Bright colours: red, royal blue or yellow make a team easy to spot at a race, festival or school trip.
  • Heather grey: a casual, soft look. Heather colours contain some polyester, which is normal and fine for most printing methods.

With 15 colours available, one colour for the whole group is usually the tidiest option. Some groups pick a second colour for organisers, volunteers or staff, so they stand out in a crowd.

Whatever you choose, check the colour on a single sample tee first. Colours can look slightly different on screen than in real life.

What else should you check before ordering plain tees in bulk?

  1. Order a sample first if colour or fit really matters.
  2. Collect sizes in one list with name, size and colour.
  3. Add one or two spares in M and L for late sign-ups.
  4. Allow time for printing if the tees go to a printer after delivery.
  5. Remember cotton can shrink a few percent, so do not size down.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum order for plain T-shirts in bulk?
Not at Marmalade Co. You can buy from 1 piece at €6.95. The quantity discount starts automatically at 3 pieces (5 % off) and rises to 10 % from 5 pieces, which is €6.26 per T-shirt. The discount applies to the product, and delivery is €7 per order.
Can I mix sizes and colours in a bulk order?
In most shops, yes, and that is how group orders usually work. Check in the basket that the quantity discount is applied to the mix you choose. The safest way to plan is to collect everyone's size and colour in a simple list first, then add them to the basket in one go.
Does Marmalade Co. deliver bulk orders to the UK?
No. Marmalade Co. currently delivers only to Ireland, Austria, Belgium, Finland, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal and Spain, by DHL for €7 flat per order. It does not currently deliver to the United Kingdom, so UK groups need a delivery address in one of those countries or a UK supplier.
How do I work out sizes for a group?
Ask everyone for their size rather than guessing. If that is not possible, a rough split for a mixed adult group is S 15 %, M 30 %, L 30 %, XL 15 % and XS or XXL 10 %. Share the size guide with chest measurements so people can check, and order one or two spares in M and L.
Which fabric weight is best for staff or event tees?
Mid-weight tees of approx. 160 to 180 gsm are a good all-round choice: comfortable for a full day and sturdy enough for regular washing and printing. For outdoor events in summer, a lighter tee is cooler. See the T-shirt gsm guide for the differences.
